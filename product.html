Material & Craft

Three Core Materials

▪
Anodized Aluminium
Aerospace-grade 6063-T5 aluminium alloy, hard-anodized to surface hardness HV≥200. Corrosion-resistant and scratch-resistant. The matte black finish is refined to the touch and communicates precision without visual aggression.
◆
Mirror Stainless Steel
Grade 304 cold-rolled stainless steel, 8K mirror-polished to Ra ≤ 0.05μm. The base acts as an optical reflective surface, extending the breathing light effect downward to create an infinite layered mirror visual.
○
Diffusing Acrylic
A full-perimeter acrylic insert at the base distributes light evenly with no hot spots. Non-load-bearing — sheet metal runs through from above to carry the structural load with all four feet anchored to the metal framework.

Climate System

Intelligent Climate Loop

A meticulously engineered convection loop integrates thermal management, ozone purification and HEPA filtration into a single closed-loop system.

1
Cool Air Ingress
Fresh cool air enters naturally through the glass door gap at the base, establishing the low-temperature foundation flow.
2
Heating Panel Convection
Cool air warms on contact with the heating panel and is drawn upward by the exhaust fan, completing the convection loop.
3
O₃ + Negative Ion Zone
Rising airflow passes through the ozone generator and negative ion module, neutralising odours and airborne pathogens.
4
Camera Heat Recovery
Waste heat from the AI camera is captured within the airflow path and exhausted simultaneously — a passive efficiency gain.
5
HEPA Final Filtration
An 80×80×25mm axial fan drives air through the H13 HEPA filter, exhausting clean air to complete the cycle at ≤28dBA.
