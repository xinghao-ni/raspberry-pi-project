Key gap: digital monitoring + community data sharing
Insight clé : Mickaël's experience bridges two core pillars of TONITRUUM's thesis — the therapeutic value of reptile-human interaction for behavioral difficulties, and the critical lack of digital infrastructure in the captive care ecosystem. His son's story directly validates the autism therapeutic application.

Problèmes & Solutions — Cadre en 6 axes
Pain points identifiés en Phase 0 → réponses design proposées
🏠
Problem 1
Husbandry difficulty
Maintaining optimal temperature, humidity, UV, and feeding schedules is complex and time-consuming — especially for multi-species enclosures.
↓
📡 Smart monitoring device
🧫
Problem 2
Parasite & disease detection
Reptile diseases are often invisible until critical. No accessible rapid-test system exists for home breeders — vet visits are costly and infrequent.
↓
🔬 Lab partenariat / téléconsultation
📦
Problem 3
Transport & care gaps
Owners traveling or hospitalised have no structured community care system. Reptile transport is stressful and legally complex across EU borders.
↓
🤝 Community care platform
💚
Problem 4
Animal welfare literacy
Public misconceptions about reptile sentience and needs lead to poor welfare outcomes. Education infrastructure is fragmented and non-standardized.
↓
📚 Media & educational content
⚖️
Problem 5
Legal & regulatory complexity
EU CITES rules, country-specific bans, and GDPR data handling create barriers. Owners lack clarity on what species they can legally keep or sell.
↓
🏛 Regulation guide & community advocacy
💼
Problem 6
Business ecosystem gap
No platform connects breeders, vets, pet stores, and enthusiasts. Individual breeders operate in isolation with no shared data, logistics, or market access.
↓
🌐 Community marketplace

TONITRUUM
IoT sensors + AI behavior layer + therapeutic bridge
Gap identifié : Every existing product is standalone hardware with no connectivity, no data layer, and no behavioral intelligence. ReptiZoo dominates with quality enclosures but offers zero digital integration — leaving owners dependent on manual observation alone. TONITRUUM targets this white space entirely.
