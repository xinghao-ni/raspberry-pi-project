Core Competitiveness — 4 Technical Pillars

Scientific feeding · Precision algorithms · Environmental control · Full-stack monitoring

🥗
Scientific Feeding Solutions
Nutrition management: customised feeding plans by species, age, and health status to ensure balanced diet
Diet control: data-driven feed type and quantity adjustment to prevent overfeeding or deficiency
🌡
Professional Environment Control
Temperature: real-time smart monitoring and adjustment to maintain optimal thermal range
Humidity: sensor + actuator system maintains appropriate moisture levels
Lighting: simulates natural light cycles with calibrated UV output for healthy development
🎯
Per-Animal Precision Algorithms
Individual ID: image recognition or RFID to accurately identify each reptile and tie data to the individual
Behaviour monitoring: video + analysis algorithms track activity in real time to assess health state
Health assessment: ML algorithms evaluate physiological and behavioural data to flag early warnings
📡
Full-Stack Habitat Monitoring
Real-time data: IoT devices continuously collect environmental and physiological readings
Data analysis: big-data processing generates care reports and personalised recommendations
Remote control: mobile and desktop app lets owners monitor and adjust habitats from anywhere

Pricing Formula & Live Calculator

Structured cost model with an interactive calculator — adjust inputs to see real-time CN/EU retail prices and gross margins

Unit Full Cost Formula
C_unit = C_mat + C_elec + C_asm + C_pkg
          + C_frt + C_war + C_NRE / N
C_NRE = one-time engineering costs (industrial design, tooling, etc.), amortised over batch size N. Larger N → lower per-unit cost.
China D2C Pricing
P_CN = C_unit × [2.3 ~ 3.2]
Target gross margin 55–65%, inclusive of D2C platform commission and marketing costs.
Europe Pricing (Full VAT Chain)
C_landed = C_unit / 7.9979 + Logistics
P_EU_ex = C_landed × [2.5 ~ 3.5]
P_EU_inc = P_EU_ex × 1.20
EU target GM 60–70% — must cover VAT, warehousing, damage rate, returns and local support costs.
Consumer Final Price
P_final = P_retail − Coupon − Launch − Bundle
Pet insurance in Europe typically covers veterinary costs only — not hardware. Consumer out-of-pocket depends primarily on retail price and promotion mechanics.
